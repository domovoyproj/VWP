# Original VWP theme artwork

The three backgrounds were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. They are static theme backgrounds, not animated wallpapers. Native generated resolution is 1672 × 941; no native 4K claim is made.

- Obsidian: premium 16:9 wallpaper, sculptural liquid glass and smoked obsidian, graphite surfaces, champagne edge light, copper reflections, detailed material surfaces, uncluttered left side, no text/UI.
- Glacier: premium 16:9 aerial glacial landscape, pale blue ice ridges, ink navy water, detailed frost, natural contours and quiet mist, uncluttered left side, no text/UI.
- Pearl: premium 16:9 macro pearl-white silk and opalescent glass, organic folds, blush reflections, warm ivory, detailed physical surfaces, uncluttered left side, no text/UI.

Catppuccin and Nord presets are VWP adaptations of their MIT-licensed palettes, not official Windows theme ports. The original VWP backgrounds are reused. Palette licenses are in `licenses/`.
