# Anime Atelier — original VWP artwork

Fifteen original environment paintings generated with the built-in imagegen tool for VWP. Individual prompts are stored beside each numbered PNG as `.prompt.txt`. No third-party characters or commercial franchise assets are used.

Each painting is 1672 × 941 pixels. The animated video is exported at 3840 × 2160 / 60 FPS; this is an upscale, not a native 4K illustration. `anime.js` animates water, clouds, environmental light, rain, petals, snow, dust and fireflies. The camera remains fixed. People and vehicles are not featured in this collection.

Themes and cursor packs are paired by `catalog.json`. Cursors are original vector drawings generated into multiresolution Windows CUR and ANI files under CC0-1.0. `tools/make_anime_styles.py` reproduces all fifteen theme and cursor packs.
