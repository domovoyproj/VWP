# Autumn & Winter

Six living wallpapers with coordinated launcher/Windows themes and complete
native cursor schemes. In the library, choose **Осень** or **Зима** in the mood
filter. Themes and cursor packs use the same names and colors.

| Wallpaper | Motion | Cursor scheme | Video |

The videos are exported at 3840 × 2160 / 60 FPS from original 1672 × 941 plates
with animated shaders and particles. Each loop lasts 12 seconds. The app creates
lighter playback copies for **Баланс** (1080p30) and **Экономия** (720p15); launcher
previews are 1280 × 720 / 30 FPS.

Each cursor scheme has 17 roles, five sizes (24–96 px), animated loading cursors
and a precise upper-left pointer hotspot. These are native Windows CUR/ANI files.

Applying a theme preserves live playback by default. **Заменить обои** applies
the theme's still poster; choose its wallpaper separately for live playback.
