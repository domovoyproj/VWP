# VWP 0.9.1 — Clean scenery

Removed the added flat people, animals, vehicles, trains and other procedural
foreground models from the illustrated wallpaper collection. This includes the
train and walking couple in Living Originals and the separate character sprites
in Midnight Record and Summer Rooftop. Rooftop Basketball stays in the library
with its original background and subtle atmosphere, without runners or a ball.

Rain, snow, water, reflections, clouds, steam, stars and environmental motion
remain. Characters painted into the original illustrations are preserved.
The actual interactive Afterlight 3D city and its star verification are unchanged.

Ten cinematic movies, three Living Originals and two Vibe scenes were rendered
afresh. The installer removes obsolete built-in sprite assets. Native verification
checks all 28 legacy overlays for changing, seamless translucent atmosphere and
absence of opaque model overlays. The catalog remains 91 records, 54 themes and
55 cursor schemes; Eco and Balance playback remain available.
